# Stopping check

*Ask this when the agent always has a "next up" and you cannot tell whether that is progress or
open-ended polishing. In the source programme this question produced the written stopping criterion, and
the author's one regret was asking it too late.*

---

Are we over-rigoring? Go through the stopping criterion in `research/KNOWLEDGE.md` §0b, item by item, for
each document, and tell me honestly:

1. Which items are met, which are not, and what specifically is missing for each unmet one.
2. What of the remaining work is *verification of things already verified*, as opposed to work that could
   still change a statement.
3. If we stopped today and published with the open points labelled as open, what would be wrong with the
   result — not suboptimal, wrong.
4. What is the single highest-value hour left, and what would we be doing after it.

If the criterion is met, say so and stop. Do not propose a next step. What remains after the criterion is
met is research or external review, and both are my decision, not a continuation of this session.
