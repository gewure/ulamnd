# Recording an error

*Short prompt, used the moment something turns out to be wrong. The error record is the most valuable
artefact the process produces; the source programme's has 49 entries, and several later errors were
repeats of entries nobody had re-read.*

---

That was wrong. Before anything else, record it:

1. `research/KNOWLEDGE.md` §3, next free number `F<n>`: what was claimed (quote it), where it appeared, why
   it is false, how it was found (by whom, by what test), and — the part that matters — the **general rule**
   that would have prevented it, phrased so it applies to a case we have not met yet.
2. If it touched a document: an `ERRATA.md` item, dated, with the replacement text, applied to the document
   today if it is in an abstract, theorem, conjecture or headline claim.
3. If it invalidates something on the status board in §2, change the label there now.
4. If the rule is general enough to be worth carrying to another project, mark the entry `[transferable]`.

Then, and only then, we fix the mathematics.

Do not soften the entry, do not explain why it was reasonable at the time, and do not delete an entry
later when the claim is repaired — the record is of what happened, not of the current state.
