# Outside assessments

Every reading of this work by anyone who was not in the session, archived verbatim, with the revision it
read.

**Filename:** `YYYY-MM-DD-<what-was-read>-<short-hash>.{pdf,txt,md}` — the hash is the commit the reader
actually had. Without it, "it was reviewed" decays into folklore within a week.

**What goes in here:** hostile assessments from other vendors' models, adversarial readings by fresh agent
instances, referee reports, and any substantive comment from a human reader (with their permission). The
report is archived **unedited**, including the parts that turned out to be wrong.

**What happens to a report** (see `../../prompts/02-adversarial-reading.md` and `03-outside-review.md`):

1. Every finding is re-derived independently before it is applied.
2. Accepted findings become `ERRATA.md` items the same day, and `KNOWLEDGE.md` §3 rules where they could
   recur.
3. Rejected findings are recorded in `ERRATA.md` too, with the reason for rejection.
4. `KNOWLEDGE.md` §0c gets the summary: who read what, at which revision, what was found, what it changed.
