# Research uses: what the findings could be good for, argued and rated

Kept separate from `KNOWLEDGE.md` (what is established and what went wrong) and from the documents (what
is claimed). **Everything here is a hint or a route, not a result, unless it says otherwise.**

| stars | meaning |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | a concrete route with the tools in hand; do it next |
| ★★★★ | promising and concrete; one identified idea missing |
| ★★★ | plausible; the connection is real but the work is open-ended |
| ★★ | speculative; worth a note, not a plan |
| ★ | essentially no route from here |
| ☆ | do not claim; listed here to prevent the claim |

**Rule for this file:** every entry names (a) what would have to be true for it to work, (b) the cheapest
test that would tell us first, and (c) an effort estimate with a range. Update ratings when something is
learned, and date the change. An entry that has been ★★★★★ for two weeks without being started is either
wrong or not actually the next thing.
