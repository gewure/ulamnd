# Literature

One entry per source. **Verification status is not optional**, and a source may not be cited in any
document until it is marked *read*.

- **read** — the source itself was opened and the relevant statement checked in it.
- **abstract only** — title and abstract seen; the statement is not verified.
- **unverified — from memory** — an agent recalled it. May be wrong in every detail, including whether it
  exists. Never cite from this state.

## Format

> **{{Author Year}}**, *{{title}}*, {{venue}}. [{{status}}, checked {{date}}]
> *States:* {{what it actually says, hypotheses included — not what it is about}}.
> *Relevance:* {{which of our statements it contains, implies, refutes, makes routine, or names
> differently}}.
> *Our claim against it:* {{what remains ours after this source is taken into account}}.
